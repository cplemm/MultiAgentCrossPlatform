SYNTHETIC PURCHASING POLICY — DEMONSTRATION ONLY

Emergency launch-recovery sourcing

1. Alternate suppliers may be used only when their quality status is Approved.
2. Split shipments are permitted for launch recovery.
3. The launch must have at least 900 units available by the committed launch date.
4. Incremental cost up to USD 30,000 is within delegated purchasing authority.
5. Any individual expedite fee must not exceed USD 12,000.
6. Options above the incremental-cost threshold require director approval.
7. Conditional or unapproved suppliers require a separate quality exception and
   must not be treated as policy-compliant without that approval.
